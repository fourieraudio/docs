# Connections

In this section, we will discuss the various ways of connecting up your **transform**.engine to the outside world.

**transform**.engine supports multiple ways of getting audio in and out of the engine.
Each selectable method is known as an **audio transport**.

Currently, both [Dante](./dante.md) and [HYPERPORT](./hyperport.md) audio transports are available;
audio transports can be configured in the [System Status](../transform.client/system/system-status.md#audio)
tab.
